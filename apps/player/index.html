Morning routine builds predictability and safety. Use the same greeting each day and invite the child to respond back in a short phrase.

Keep it joyful and brief: a warm greeting, a choice (“Would you like to watch a short song?”), and a simple transition ritual ("Let’s go. One, two, three!").

Celebrate attempts, not perfection. Echo back what you hear and model the target phrase once.
